BoardBridge

BoardBridge is a publishing tool for the owner's own Pinterest business accounts. It creates standard image pins through the official Pinterest API — never through browser automation, saved passwords, or screen scraping.

What it does

  • Publishes only pins the owner has explicitly approved, one by one, from an approval queue.
  • Creates a pin with its title, description, destination link, board, and image — exactly as approved.
  • Spreads publishing through the day and never re-posts the identical pin to many boards in a burst.

What it does not do

  • It does not publish for other people or other accounts.
  • It does not collect, sell, or share anyone's personal data.
  • It does not post, comment, follow, or message on anyone's behalf.

Who it is for

BoardBridge is built for a single publisher: the owner of the connected Pinterest business accounts, publishing their own content to their own boards. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

How it connects

Each Pinterest business account is connected once by its owner through Pinterest's own OAuth consent screen. BoardBridge stores that account's access and refresh tokens securely, refreshes them before they expire, and never asks for or stores a Pinterest password.
