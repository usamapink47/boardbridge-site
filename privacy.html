Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026
App: BoardBridge
Website: https://prismatic-swan-42d2cb.netlify.app/privacy.html
Contact: usamapink47@gmail.com

BoardBridge is a publishing tool for a single publisher: the owner of the connected Pinterest business accounts. It is not a public service and it has no other users. This policy explains what data the app accesses, why, and how it is protected.

1. What data the app accesses

Through the official Pinterest API, and only after the account owner approves the connection on Pinterest's own OAuth consent screen, the app accesses:

  • Account information — basic profile details of the connected business account, used to confirm which account a pin belongs to.
  • Boards — the account's board list (names and IDs), used only to place an approved pin on the board the owner chose.
  • Pins — pins the app itself creates for the account, and pin data needed to check for duplicates before publishing.

The app does not access private messages, followers' personal data, analytics about other people, or any data from accounts that have not been connected by their owner. It never asks for, receives, or stores a Pinterest password.

2. Why the data is used

The data above is used for one purpose only: publishing pins that the account owner has explicitly approved, to the board the owner chose, on the account the owner connected. No other use is made of it.

3. Permissions (scopes) requested

The app requests exactly these Pinterest API permissions, and no others:

  • boards:read — read the account's boards.
  • boards:write — create boards only if the owner asks for it.
  • pins:read — read pins the app created, to avoid duplicates.
  • pins:write — create approved pins.
  • user_accounts:read — read basic account information.

Adding a permission later would require the owner to approve it again through Pinterest's consent screen.

4. How tokens are stored and refreshed

  • Each connected account has its own access token and refresh token pair. Tokens are stored server-side in a file with restricted permissions, are never printed to logs, and are never sent anywhere except to Pinterest's own API.
  • Access tokens expire after 30 days; refresh tokens expire after 60 days. The app refreshes tokens automatically before they expire, so the owner does not need to re-connect routinely.
  • If a token is revoked, leaked, or the owner changes their Pinterest password, that account must be re-connected through Pinterest's consent screen.

5. What the app publishes, and what it never does

The app publishes only pins from an approval queue that a human has explicitly approved, one pin at a time. It never publishes automatically without that approval, never posts the identical pin to many boards in a burst, never comments, follows, messages, or acts on anyone else's account, and never uses browser automation or saved passwords.

6. Sharing and sale of data

Data accessed by the app is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, for advertising or any other purpose. The only external service the app talks to is Pinterest's own API, to perform the publishing the owner approved. This website itself uses no tracking scripts, no advertising, and no third-party analytics.

7. Retention

Tokens and account connection details are kept only while the account stays connected and the app is in use. Pin content (titles, descriptions, links, images) is kept only as long as needed to publish approved pins and to check for duplicates. When an account is disconnected, its tokens and connection details are deleted (see section 8).

8. Deletion and disconnection

The owner can disconnect an account at any time in either of these ways:

  • Revoke BoardBridge's access in their Pinterest account settings; or
  • Contact us at usamapink47@gmail.com and ask for the account to be disconnected and its stored tokens and connection details to be deleted.

On disconnection, stored tokens for that account are deleted. Pins already published to Pinterest remain on Pinterest unless the owner deletes them there; deleting them from Pinterest is outside this app's control.

9. Security

Tokens are stored with restricted file permissions and are never included in logs, emails, or chat messages. App secrets are stored separately from the code and are never published. If a token is believed to be leaked, it should be revoked in Pinterest's settings and the account re-connected.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will be posted on this page before the change takes effect for any new publishing. Material changes to what data is accessed will require the owner to approve the connection again.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or a deletion request: usamapink47@gmail.com.
